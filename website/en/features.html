Track your money

Every account, card and file lands in one list, categorised the way you taught it.

Bank and card sync

Your accounts and cards in one list, updated every day.

  • 18 Israeli banks and card companies
  • A daily sync on a schedule, or whenever you ask
  • One-time codes from the bank, typed in the app
  • Open banking (XS2A) with an access token instead of a password
  • Bank passwords encrypted on your computer with AES‑256‑GCM
Full page →

Files and import

Anything a bank or your old app can export comes in.

  • CSV, XLSX and XLS statements
  • A watched folder that imports whatever you drop into it
  • Smart sheet: paste a table, fix any cell, undo the whole import
  • A PDF statement or a photo of one, read by your AI
  • Duplicates caught per account, so nothing counts twice
Full page →

Transactions and rules

Every shekel in one list, sorted the way you taught it.

  • Grouped by week, with search, filters and bulk edit
  • Splits, tags and an owner for each transaction
  • Rules set category, tags and owner on every sync
  • Merchant memory and a built-in dictionary of Israeli merchants
  • Receipts attached, cash added in seconds, CSV and OFX export

Plan and analyse

Budgets, forecasts, pension, investments and net worth, built on the same transactions.

Budgets and trackers

Budgets that show the pace, not just the total.

  • A budget per category, built from your average in one click
  • Pace through the month, so trouble shows early
  • Envelopes and a 50/30/20 view
  • Trackers for a period, like eating out until month end
  • Summaries by month, quarter, half-year and year
Full page →

Forecasts and insights

What will be left, and what is on its way.

  • A balance forecast for each bank account, with a range
  • Recurring charges and subscriptions, found automatically
  • Remaining installments and upcoming obligations
  • Planned expenses that match themselves to the real charge
  • Spending anomalies, and why spending changed per merchant

Money on the table

One yearly figure for everything you could save.

  • Bank fees, overdraft interest and FX markup
  • Fund fees above the market, and overlapping insurance
  • Idle cash and the donation tax credit
  • A next step for each; what you mark done counts as saved
  • A financial health score with one list of what needs care

Pension and savings

Pension, study funds and provident funds, next to everyday money.

  • Balances from Clal, Harel, Meitav, Menora and Migdal
  • New: Altshuler Shaham and Phoenix
  • Import your agent's report (JSON): products, fees, deposits and insurance
  • Your management fees against the market
Full page →

Investments and markets

Holdings next to everyday money, in any currency.

  • Holdings with buys and sells
  • New: Excellence Trade brokerage balance
  • Allocation by type and currency, against the S&P 500 and TA-125
  • Indices, currencies, commodities and fund prices
  • Private ventures with invested, outstanding and realized gain

Net worth and goals

Where the household stands, and where it's heading.

  • Net worth over time, from assets and liabilities
  • Loans with their progress, and your credit score over time
  • Savings goals, with a check that they all fit
  • Financial independence: when investments can cover spending
  • A lifetime money model to age 90

Insurance and family document

Every policy, and the document someone will need one day.

  • Policies with coverage, excess, renewal date and agent
  • What you actually paid for each one in the last year
  • Payments from the bank matched to their policy
  • A double-insurance check
  • A family document for the day someone else needs to find everything

Israeli money tools

25+ calculators, each with the year its figures are valid for.

  • Tax refund and credit points, with a Form 135 checklist
  • Mortgage under Bank of Israel rules, rent or buy, car cost
  • Severance, annuity or lump sum, pension track choice
  • Benefits check, forgotten money, bank fees against the basic track
  • A knowledge center, daily tips and life-stage checklists

Pension and investments, next to everyday money.

Balances sync from eight pension funds and investment houses, so net worth stops being a once-a-year letter. See your management fees against the market and the whole household's net worth over time.

  • Altshuler Shaham New
  • Phoenix New
  • Excellence Trade New
  • Clal

  • Migdal
  • Import your agent's report (JSON): products, fees, deposits and the insurance in it
  • Investments with buys and sells, allocation by type and currency, compared with the S&P 500 and TA-125
  • 25+ calculators for tax refunds, pension tracks, mortgage under Bank of Israel rules and financial independence

AI, family and your data

Help on your terms, a Telegram bot and profiles for the whole household. All of it stays with you.

AI and MCP

Optional help, running where you choose.

  • Local with Ollama, with model downloads inside the app
  • Claude, or a CLI agent you already have, such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI
  • Sorts what your rules and the dictionary don't know
  • Ask your money questions in plain words
  • A read-only MCP server for AI assistants

Telegram bot

A private bot that answers only your own chat.

  • A summary on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday mornings
  • New expenses after each sync, with what's left in their budget
  • Reminders before charges, and alerts when a bank stops syncing
  • Sort transactions and save a forwarded card-charge SMS
  • A snapshot or a sync on request; amounts can be hidden
Full page →

Household and profiles

One household, and everyone gets their own screen.

  • Profiles in a "who's using?" screen, each with its own layout
  • Splitting with people, with "paid it all back" in one tap
  • A business share per transaction, and a business report
  • Monthly recap, guided reviews, your journey and a well-being check
  • Pip, the household coach: alerts, insights and ranked advice
  • An accessibility menu (Alt+A), saved per profile

Privacy and security

Your data stays in one file on your computer.

  • Listens only on 127.0.0.1 and blocks requests from other sites
  • Bank passwords encrypted with AES‑256‑GCM
  • App lock with a passphrase, PIN or passkey
  • Privacy mode hides every amount (Ctrl/⌘+Shift+P)
  • Encrypted automatic backup, restore, full export and wipe
Full page →

Desktop, Docker and phone

On your computer, your home server, and your phone at home.

  • A Windows and Mac app that updates itself on Windows
  • A background service that starts with the computer
  • Docker, with a backup first and rollback on a bad update
  • Your phone on the home Wi-Fi, installable to the home screen
  • Workspaces with sign-in, and viewer or editor permissions

AI if you want it, on your terms.

Everything works without AI. Turn it on and it sorts the transactions your rules and the merchant dictionary don't know, and answers questions like "how much did we spend on food last month?" in plain words.

  • Local with Ollama: nothing leaves the computer, and models download from inside the app
  • MCP server so Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client can ask about your money, read-only
  • Cloud AI costs a fraction of a shekel per sync, paid straight to the provider
  • OllamaOn your computer
  • ClaudeWith your API key
  • CLI agentsClaude Code, Codex, Gemini and more
  • No AIDictionary and your rules

Connect an AI assistant over MCP

claude mcp add penny -- node /path/to/Penny/scripts/mcp.mjs
